BI Documentation Generator

This is a DEVELOPMENT BUILD. It is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you
before it runs. Production releases are signed.

What it installs: the generator (command line bidoc.exe and the desktop app) for your
Windows account only. No administrator rights are needed.

What it does not install:
- Power BI Desktop and pbi-tools. They are needed only for .pbix files. Install them
  separately, then record pbi-tools with: bidoc config --pbi-tools C:\path\to\pbi-tools.exe
  PBIP projects, TMDL/BIM models, PBIR reports and Data Factory exports work without them.
- The Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, needed by the desktop window. It is part of current
  Windows 10 and 11 installations.

Your data: history, settings and temporary workspaces are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\bidoc.
Upgrading keeps them. Uninstalling keeps them unless you choose to delete them. Generated
documentation in your output folders is never deleted.
